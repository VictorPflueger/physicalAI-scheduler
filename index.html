How the prototype works

312 passengers are about to go to the wrong platform

An AI agent stands between the operational timetable, which operations keep, and what passengers are told. It finds where the two disagree, has an engine count who is misled and drafts the fix. You decide.

This demo: a platform change at Köln Hbf
The leadA radio message: ICE 618 leaves from another platform, the app still says the old one
The sourcePlatform 7 since 07:21, after a points failure
What passengers are toldPlatform 5, in the app, on the displays, in the announcements and in the coach order
If nothing is done312 passengers go to the wrong platform, the first in 14 min

What the agent does, step by step

The agent reads the source and every channel, asks the track's sensors, lets an engine count who is misled, proposes three responses and drafts the one you approve. Four figures say what that is worth.

What is real and what is invented

The stations and lines are real. Trains, times, passengers and sensor readings are invented. In this replay the agent's words are scripted, and an engine computes every figure.

Where everything comes from
ScriptedThe agent's words in the replay, played through its real tools
ComputedEvery passenger and every minute, by a deterministic engine from the operating snapshot; the AI recommends one of the costed options, and you decide
A live runStart agent runs Claude Sonnet 5.5 on your own API key. You are interacting with an AI system
Nothing is publishedDrafts stay drafts, approved or not: the page is connected to no railway system

Show it in five minutes

The story plays by itself and stops where you decide.

  1. Spot: it plays by itself; point at the four figures. “312 passengers go to the wrong platform, the first in 14 min.” 1 min
  2. Check: point at the sensors. “Every channel says platform 5, and the interlocking confirms platform 7.” 1 min
  3. Calculate: point at the first estimate. “The app alone looked like 8 passengers; every channel together, 312.” 1 min
  4. Propose: press Approve option A. “Re-publishing from the source leaves 7; a person decides in about 4 min.” 1 min
  5. Prepare: press Approve both drafts. “Nothing is published. Live, the agent's run costs about €0.09.” 1 min
